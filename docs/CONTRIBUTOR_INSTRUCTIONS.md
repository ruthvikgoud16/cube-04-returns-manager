# For the person with the product

Open the one Drive link you were sent.

You are assigned one product number, such as PRODUCT 07.

Open that folder only.

Open PRODUCT DETAILS and fill in the product you physically have.

Then open PHOTOS and put one real photo in each folder:

01 — FRONT
02 — BACK
03 — IDENTIFICATION
04 — ACCESSORIES
05 — CONDITION, only if something visible is worn or damaged

Do not edit any other number.

Do not use pictures from the internet or pictures made by an AI tool.

Do not hide damage and do not create damage.

If you do not know a SKU or ASIN, write UNKNOWN.

You do not decide restock, refurbish, liquidate, or dispose.

The order id in the folder is a project label, not your Amazon order.
