# Customer letter

This is a hypothesis about a prep-center owner. It is not a record of a conversation, and it is not evidence of demand.

You open a return and decide in a few seconds whether it goes back to stock. Two people can grade the same scuff differently, and next month nobody can show what actually arrived. The expensive miss is restocking a broken or substituted unit, and the quiet miss is liquidating one that could have been sold.

What I would pay for is a record I can open later: the photos, what was ordered, what was visible, and who overrode the recommendation. I would not pay for a system that stamps restock on a photo it cannot read.

If this letter is wrong about what a prep center would rank first, the letter is doing its job.
